# Audit — October 2026 (Phase 1: findings only, nothing changed yet)

Scope: data → features → models → rules → paper trading, as the code runs today. Each finding cites
the code. No backtests or training were run for this report, so there are no new metrics here; the
numbers quoted come from earlier GitHub research runs.

## Where things stand (from earlier runs)
- **Long-term** (momentum 90% + model 10%, 5 holdings, weekly): walk-forward 2015–2026 earned 23.7%/yr
  after costs (Sharpe 0.91, deflated Sharpe 97.8%) vs Nifty 9%. Falls of up to ~50%; −31% in COVID.
  **Not yet tested free of survivorship bias.**
- **Intraday** (both books): about +0.05% per trade before costs vs 0.32% round-trip cost. **No net
  edge.** Live paper results agree (5 Oct: −₹412 and −₹482 per ₹1 lakh).
- **F&O paper** (new 22 Oct): no history and no backtest, so it is paper-only learning.

## Defects, by priority

| # | Severity | Where | Evidence | Minimal fix | Retrain? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **High — validity** | Long-term backtests, `backtest/run.point_in_time_universe` | The universe is today's Nifty 250 projected back in time: stocks that fell out or were delisted are missing, so the 23.7%/yr is optimistic by an unknown amount. Bhavcopy files 2005→ are now on the `market-data` branch. | Run `scripts/research_survivorship.py` (research.yml, GitHub) on the point-in-time top-N-by-turnover universe from bhav. | No (test only) |
| 2 | **High — validity** | `backtest/intraday.trade_exits` (l.95) | Stop-loss exits fill exactly at the stop price. A gap or fast move through the stop fills worse, so rule tuning and backtests overstate profits. | Fill at the worse of the stop price and the bar's open/low (high for shorts) in the bar where the stop is hit. | No (rules re-tune nightly) |
| 3 | **High — overfitting** | `models/trainer.tune_rules` (l.405–443) and nightly tuning | The 60-day "holdout" rolls forward every night, so its days are re-checked every night (~250 looks a year), and nightly hyperparameter tuning promotes on paired t ≥ 2 with no count of trials, so false promotions build up. | Freeze the holdout window for a month at a time; log every trial and require a deflated threshold (t rises with the number of trials). | No |
| 4 | **Medium — realism** | `paper/fno.py` | Options are filled at the last traded price with no bid-ask spread. On cheap stock options (e.g. a ₹2.85 premium, ₹0.05 tick) the spread is 2–5% each way. Futures margin is a flat 20% guess. | Add a half-spread cost for options (max of 1 tick and 1% of premium); margin from Angel's margin API if available, else per-stock (20–40%). | No |
| 5 | **Medium — operations** | Mac (`launchd` service) | The Mac slept during market hours on 6 and 22 Oct, so the 9:46 picks were missed or late. `caffeinate -s` only works on charger power. | User action: keep it plugged in, or run `sudo pmset -c sleep 0`. | No |
| 6 | **Medium — operations** | `live/intraday_bars.first30` | Angel One throttles the 250 candle requests, which took 10–30 min on restart days. Mitigated: 2 passes, then Yahoo (loses the 1-minute features). | Fine as is; monitor. | No |
| 7 | **Medium — risk policy** | `paper/intraday.get_rules` (`id_every_day`) | At your request the skip rules are off, so the books trade on weak days, which backtests showed lose more. This is a deliberate trade-off, recorded here. | Keep for learning; don't take it to real money. | No |
| 8 | Low | `models/engine.model_inputs` | Missing model inputs are silently NaN-filled with a warning. This is how 8 long-term inputs went missing live for weeks (fixed 5 Oct). | Make a missing input a health-banner error and a test failure, not just a warning. | No |

## Already in place (verified in code)
- Long-term walk-forward with an embargo between train and test (`models/longterm.walk_forward`, `EMBARGO_DAYS`).
- Intraday look-ahead test (`tests/test_features_intraday.py`), previous-day adjustment factor in `align_to_daily`.
- Costs: delivery and intraday charges, slippage from the first-30-minute range, liquidity cap (1% of 30-minute volume), 1% risk per trade.
- Kill switches: stale data, stale prices, broken scores, weekly loss 3%, daily loss 1.5%, long-term drawdown 25%; one open long-term position per stock (database guard).
- No code path places real orders (`data/angelone.py` is market-data only).

## Recommended order
1. **#1 survivorship test** (GitHub, no code change). It decides whether the long-term edge is real.
2. **#2 stop fills** and **#4 option spreads**: small fixes that make every backtest and paper result more honest.
3. **#3 holdout freeze and trial counting.**
4. #8, then #5/#6 monitoring.

Expect results to look **worse** after #1, #2 and #4. That is the point: they remove optimism.
